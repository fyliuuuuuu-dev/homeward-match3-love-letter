# The Companions

Homeward keeps its rules neutral, but its little bit world has two resident supervisors.

## Dabing, Chief Comfort Officer

Dabing is a purple-gold shaded shorthair with a low center of gravity and a steady presence. He represents the moment a trip ends, the door closes, and home begins to feel still again. His department considers comfort a core infrastructure concern.

## Yuwan, Head of Quality Assurance

Yuwan is a silver-white longhair with golden-green eyes, an alert expression, and a talent for noticing whatever everyone else missed. She represents curiosity, attention to detail, and the sensible decision to check one more time.

Together they supervised the project and approved nothing on the first try. If software gets ghosts, may these be kind ones.

## Public artwork boundary

The two displayed portraits are approved AI-redrawn personal character artwork. They are the only personal character images published in this repository. Source photographs, reference materials, editable production files, alternate sizes, and process records are not included.

The game stores only neutral companion identifiers, A and B. A fork can replace the portraits or names without changing rules or save semantics. The interface also falls back to letter badges when either image is missing.
